How it works

Every score is an estimate, not a verdict. It weighs text patterns (~80 AI-isms), sentence structure, author/byline, and URL shape — all computed locally on the title and snippet already shown on the page. It never fetches article bodies, and it will miss lightly-edited AI text and sometimes flag formal human prose. Treat it as a first-pass hint.
